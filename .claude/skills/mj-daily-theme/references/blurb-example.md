# Daily Theme blurb — house voice (as printed in MJ 43, "Solar eclipse")

Two paragraphs, ~150 words. The first is about the subject itself: plain,
informative, concrete facts a reader may not know (true ones only). The second
turns to the community: what they made of it, then "In one image, … In
another, …" describing real images from the selection. No hype, no
exclamation marks. Daniel edits these himself; this is the version that ran.
Also read whatever blurb is on the daily-theme spread in the current doc.

> On August 12, 2026, a total solar eclipse swept across Greenland, Iceland, and Spain. As the moon's path crosses that of the sun, the Earth is briefly enveloped in an eerie darkness. Animals get confused: birds go quiet, crickets begin chirping, and cattle head back to the barn as if night had come early. The whole thing lasts a few minutes at most, and then the light comes back and everyone carries on.
>
> It's hard to articulate the strangeness of a sudden, brief night, but the Midjourney community succeeded. This month's selection conveys the unsettling, otherworldly feeling of being in the path of totality. In one image, a starved black dog stands on a rock beneath the corona. In another, the solar event is spelled, like crosshairs, across a girl's face.
